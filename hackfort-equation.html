The Hackfort Equation

A quantum criticality index: how much of a rate is carried by the quantum world
Sabrina Hackfort · Independent Researcher · ORCID 0009-0007-7458-2487
❦ ❦ ❦

Classical models describe chemistry as particles climbing over energy barriers. Yet protons and hydrides can also tunnel through them, and coherence can shape how a reaction proceeds. The Hackfort Equation condenses this into a single dimensionless number, the quantum criticality index ΦH. It states what share of a system's behaviour comes from quantum effects.

Axiom 1 — Quantum Discrepancy (Hackfort, 2026)

For every system S possessing at least one quantum-mechanical degree of freedom, there exists an observable 𝒪 such that the quantum-mechanical expectation value exceeds the classical one:

⟨𝒪⟩QM − ⟨𝒪⟩class ≥ 0.

The Equation

ΦH(S, t) = Σi wi · αi(t) · ηi · (1 + |λ̃i(t)|)
ηi = 1 − 1/κi · κi = kQM / kclass · λ̃i(t) = (d/dt ln Γi) / λref

ηi — the quantum fraction. The share of a channel's rate that comes from quantum tunneling: 0 for a purely classical channel, approaching 1 when the quantum path dominates.

αi(t) — functionality. How active the channel is: 1 for a healthy enzyme, below 1 when inhibited, towards 0 when lost.

λ̃i(t) — coherence dynamics. How fast the quantum landscape changes, derived from the Lindblad master equation for open quantum systems.

wi — weight. The relative importance of each channel, with Σwi = 1.

The structure is multiplicative, and this encodes a physical statement: without a quantum effect (η = 0) there is no quantum criticality, whatever the dynamics. Coherence can amplify quantum behaviour, but it cannot create it from nothing.

Properties. For channels with κi ≥ 1:

  1. ΦH ≥ 0.
  2. ΦH is dimensionless, since every factor is a ratio.
  3. ΦH = 0 if and only if every channel is classical or inactive.
  4. ΦH < maxi (1 + |λ̃i|).

The Heart: the Quantum Fraction η

Everything rests on η, and η rests on κ, the ratio of the rate with tunneling to the rate without it, both in the same model. Scope and Corrections

Science moves forward by correcting itself, so the record is kept open here. κ must be the ratio of rates with and without tunneling in the same model. It is not the kinetic isotope effect. An earlier version of this page used isotope effects as κ, and those values are withdrawn. Quantum effects can also lower a rate, so η as defined applies to channels with κ ≥ 1. The earlier pharmacological corollary, that drug doses are wrong by a factor κ, is withdrawn as well. Clinical doses rest on measured clearance, which already contains any tunneling. The real lever is deliberate deuteration. Its strategy tool ZENO-DX, tested on the approved drug deutetrabenazine with measured inputs only, predicts one of the two active metabolites correctly and underestimates the other; an earlier, more favourable version of this test is withdrawn. The corollaries on biodiversity and on cryptographic systems remain hypotheses that have not yet been tested.

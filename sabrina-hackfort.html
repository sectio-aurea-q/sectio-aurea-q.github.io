Plate I. A wave meets a barrier it cannot climb. Part of it arrives on the other side anyway.
❦

Classical chemistry pictures a reaction as a climb over an energy barrier. Yet protons and hydrides can also pass through it. The Hackfort Equation does not replace this physics — it measures it: what share of a rate is carried by the quantum tunnel, condensed into one dimensionless number.

Axiom 1 — Quantum Discrepancy (Hackfort, 2026)

For every system S possessing at least one quantum-mechanical degree of freedom, there exists an observable 𝒪 such that

⟨𝒪⟩QM ≠ ⟨𝒪⟩class.

Furthermore, the discrepancy is non-negative and systematic:

Δ(S) ≡ ⟨𝒪⟩QM − ⟨𝒪⟩class ≥ 0.
As stated in the original manuscript of 29 March 2026.
ΦH(S, t) = Σi wi · αi(t) · ηi · (1 + |λ̃i(t)|)
ηi = 1 − 1/κi  ·  κi = kQM/kclass  ·  λ̃i = (d/dt ln Γi)/λref

Its heart is η, the quantum fraction: zero for a purely classical channel, close to one when the tunnel carries nearly everything. So far the equation has been checked against independent data on one enzyme step — formate dehydrogenase, where four fifths of the rate run through the tunnel (η = 0.80 at 300 K, QM/MM). Extensions to biodiversity and cryptography remain open hypotheses, not results.

RADIX RIEMANNIANA
Number Theory · radix-rank
The reproducing kernel of the Weil form, built from the primes alone. Chooses, for each elliptic curve, the optimal test function for bounding its analytic rank. Every bound certified with ball arithmetic.

MEG-APSU
Quantum Enzyme Classifier
Classifies FDA-target enzymes as quantum-critical or classical using kinetic isotope effects and tunneling transmission coefficients. 35.7% of 115 FDA targets identified as quantum-critical.

TERRA
Quantum Biodiversity Scanner
A survey of all 10,774 IUCN Critically Endangered species for enzymes that rely on quantum tunneling: IUCN → UniProt → PDB → MEG-APSU Lindblad solver. About one in nine species carries at least one such enzyme.
v0.5.0 · 1,424 quantum-critical enzymes across 1,216 species · read the honest numbers →
ZENO
Tunneling & Deuteration Toolkit
Computes the quantum fraction η = 1 − 1/κ of the Hackfort Equation (Eckart tunneling). ZENO-DX predicts the real benefit of deuterating a drug after metabolic switching. Successor of NANSHE, whose dosing-error claim is withdrawn.
Checked against 4 published QM/MM enzyme studies · η 0.50–0.94
